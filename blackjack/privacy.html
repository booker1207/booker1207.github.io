Privacy Policy

Blackjack (iOS) · Effective 29 October 2026

Blackjack is a practice game that teaches blackjack basic strategy under the rules of real casinos. It is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

No account, no servers of our own

The app has no sign-up and we run no server for it. Your chips, level, statistics, chosen casino and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We do not see or collect this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for free chips, and occasional full-screen ads between rounds. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

On iOS the app asks for permission before your Advertising Identifier is used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google asks for your choices first, and you can change them later from the app's settings (Ad privacy options).

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Chip packs, "No ads forever" and the VIP subscription are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only reads which purchases are active on your device so it can unlock them. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Chips have no real-world value

Chips are virtual, for practice only. They cannot be cashed out, transferred or exchanged for money or anything of value. The app offers no real-money gambling.

Location

The app does not ask for or use your device's location.

Children

The app is a simulated casino game intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: peanuts0776@gmail.com.
